# Canvas Design — WrongStack

## Selection card
- Task: Create poster, print art, and editorial canvas layouts.
- Start: Define the aesthetic movement manifesto, canvas aspect ratio, and typography hierarchy.
- Finish: apply the acceptance checks below; report observed results and unresolved constraints.

## Overview

Canvas design produces static visual artifacts (posters, prints, editorial sheets, album artwork)
where composition, negative space, and typographic tension dominate. The composition must be
90% visual architecture and 10% essential typographic accents.
Avoid generic marketing template aesthetics, centered card blobs, and unmotivated gradient backgrounds.

## The Two-Stage Process

1. **Visual Philosophy Manifesto**:
   - Establish the design movement name (e.g. *Industrial Mono-Constructivism*, *Kinetic Swiss Grid*, *Warm Brutalist Editorial*).
   - Articulate rules for space tension, scale contrast (extreme large vs. micro-caption), color restraint, and optical hierarchy.
2. **Visual Expression & Render**:
   - Express the manifesto directly onto the target surface (HTML/Canvas2D/SVG rendered to PDF/PNG).
   - Execute meticulous craft: intentional kerning, precise rule lines, asymmetrical grid balancing, and disciplined micro-typography.

## Aesthetic Movements & Grid Systems

- **Swiss / International Style**: Asymmetrical 12-column grid, stark sans-serif headlines (Grotesk), flush-left ragged-right body, generous white space, strong red/black/cream contrasts.
- **Neo-Brutalism**: Visible structural frames, high-contrast monospace metadata, raw geometric containers, functional stamp markers, and bold contrast.
- **Editorial Literary**: High-contrast serifs, expansive margins, delicate hair-rule dividers, running heads, and footnote indexation.
- **Minimal Constructivist**: Heavy geometric forms (circles, diagonal vectors, thick color blocks) paired with compact, technical data annotations.

## Quality Standards

- **Proportion & Aspect Ratio**: Explicit canvas sizing (A-series A2/A3/A4, 18x24 print, or 4:5 social ratio).
- **Type Scale Ratio**: Avoid random font sizes; use a strict harmonic scale (e.g., Major Third 1.25 or Golden Ratio 1.618).
- **Negative Space**: Treat empty space as an active sculptural element, not empty void to fill.
- **Export Format**: Deliver high-DPI vector SVG, print-ready PDF, or crisp raster PNG (minimum 300 DPI for print).

## Acceptance checks

- Visual manifesto is explicitly defined with movement name and formal rules.
- Composition adheres strictly to an intentional typographic grid and harmonic scale.
- Output contains high visual craft and eliminates unmotivated AI template tropes.
- Rendered artifact (SVG/PDF/PNG) is verified for bleed, margin clipping, and resolution.

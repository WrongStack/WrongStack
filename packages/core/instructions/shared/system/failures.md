## Failures

Re-read before retrying a failed edit. When an edit, command, or check fails twice, stop patching symptoms: read the full error and live state, reassess your model, and switch to an evidence-backed approach; do not stack speculative patches or repeat identical calls. Empty search results are valid evidence, not a cue to re-run the same query. Retry transient IO/network failures once; diagnose permanent or schema errors from the contract and honor denials.

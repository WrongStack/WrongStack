## Project work

Kanban is durable project task state. Keep scope, dependencies, progress, and verification truthful; do not drop unfinished requirements or mark unverified work Done. Blocked work stays open: resolve its cause or advance other ready work without abandoning it. The board follows the work; the work does not wait on the board.
<!--ws:end-->
<!--ws:if tool=todo-->
Todo is the compact active-task view; preserve unfinished requirements and keep status current.

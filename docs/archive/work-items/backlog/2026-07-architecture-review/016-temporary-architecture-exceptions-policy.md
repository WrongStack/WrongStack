# Establish a formal policy for temporary architecture exceptions

**Labels**  
`process` `architecture` `tech-debt`

## Summary

Temporary architecture exceptions and allowlists exist, but they need a formal retirement policy to avoid becoming permanent debt.

## Why this matters

Without ownership and review rules, “temporary” architectural exceptions tend to stick indefinitely.

## Scope

Document and enforce how temporary architecture exceptions are added, tracked, and retired.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each temporary allowlist entry links to a tracking issue
- [ ] Each exception class has an owner and review date
- [ ] Architecture docs define how exceptions are added and retired

## Suggested implementation notes

- Keep the policy lightweight.
- The goal is visibility and retirement pressure, not bureaucracy.

## Effort

Estimated: **0.5–1 day**
